Antarctica: ice, life and research

2 October 2026, Fri, 16:10 Letovo School, Winter Garden

How the icy continent formed, why it matters so much for science and how polar explorers live and work today. The talk covers microorganisms that survive in extreme conditions, the discovery of Antarctica by Russian sailors, the conquest of the South Pole and the modern exploration of the continent. We will also describe how research is done today, how polar explorers travel, live and work at stations, and about the harsh nature of Antarctica. The talk shows the continent as a unique scientific laboratory and an example of human courage. There is no recording: the speaker asked us not to share details of the talk through external channels.

SpeakerZorigto Namsaraev

Head of the synthetic biology laboratory, Kurchatov Institute
